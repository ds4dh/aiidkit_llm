Unified Post-Transplant Outcome & Infection Risk Assessment

Quantitative decision support for kidney transplant recipients. Longitudinal clinical event sequences are encoded to estimate calibrated multi-horizon risk (30 days to 5 years) across bacterial, viral, and fungal pathogens, allograft loss, and mortality.
