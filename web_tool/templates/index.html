Welcome to AIIDKIT Decision Support

An AI decision support tool that predicts post-transplant bacterial infection risk to assist clinical teams.

Quick Start Guide:
  1. Enter Patient Data: Use the right sidebar to add clinical events or select an example patient (High risk / Low risk).
  2. Analyse Patient: Click Analyse patient to compute infection risk scores across 30, 60, and 90-day time horizons.
  3. Review Insights: Inspect clinical risk drivers, compare cohort positioning, and explore risk subgroup profiles.
